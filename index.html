02 · Aboutanalyst summary
Analyst summaryfirst person

I work the defensive side of security operations. Three and a half years of it: two years running SIEM and endpoint security across 50+ managed systems at Microland in Bengaluru, then consulting on security operations at Nurix Therapeutics while finishing a master’s at Maryland.

The part of the pipeline I care about is the unglamorous middle — where a log becomes an alert and an alert becomes a decision. Most of what a SIEM emits is noise, and tuning it down is slower and less celebrated than shipping a new rule, but it is the whole difference between a SOC that investigates and a SOC that has learned to ignore itself. At Nurix that has meant rewriting KQL detections for fidelity rather than coverage, and designing Conditional Access policies around how people actually authenticate instead of how the documentation assumes they do.

I spend time on the offensive side too — ROP chains, RSA key weaknesses, C2 framework behavior — not to pivot into red teaming, but because you cannot write credible detection for a technique you have never executed. Every exploit lab I have finished has ended in a detection rule.

I am looking for full-time work in security operations, detection engineering, or security architecture.
